# v1.0.0: Final Report, Guarantees, and Reproducible Walkthrough (M6)

Milestone M6 completes the extended evaluation of the Dapr Distributed
Fulfillment PoC. The release brings together durable order acceptance,
inventory reservation, payment and shipping simulators, operator
intervention, and event recovery. It uses synthetic data for local
evaluation. [Previous release](v0.6.0.md)

## What It Does

- **Final report.** Answers the original evaluation questions, including
  where Dapr Workflow helped, which guarantees the application enforces
  in SQL, what the stack costs to run, and how stock actors compared
  with SQL-only coordination. [Final report](../final-report.md)
- **Guarantees and evidence.** Maps each business invariant and
  operational guarantee to its enforcement mechanism and the scenarios
  or drills that exercised it. It also identifies gaps in the evidence.
  [Guarantees](../guarantees.md)
- **Guided walkthrough.** Six checks cover build identity, end-to-end
  fulfillment, idempotent replay through another replica, cross-replica
  approval, approval while both workflow hosts are down, and cancellation
  while an order waits for review. [Walkthrough](../walkthrough.md)
- **Kubernetes smoke test.** The Kustomize template passed on a local
  kind cluster with the `v0.6.0` release images. The checks covered
  migrations, app and sidecar readiness, and one completed order.
  [Kubernetes template](../../deploy/kubernetes/README.md)
- **Replica startup fix.** Order API and Inventory serialize creation
  of their runtime-probe diagnostics tables with SQL application locks.
  This covers the race found when replicas started together on a fresh
  database. [Journal](../journal.md#two-replicas-raced-to-create-the-diagnostics-tables)

## Verified Guarantees

The resilience suite ran on Docker Compose with two or three replicas
per role and up to five compute replicas.

| Guarantee | Evidence |
|---|---|
| A retried submission produces one order, and a late workflow start produces no additional effect | S04, S24, S43, R02 |
| Inventory stays within stock limits under contention, including actor relocation | S03 in actor and SQL-only modes, R04, R05, S20 |
| An accepted approval survives loss of the workflow hosts, and the order progresses once | W5, R20, S52 |
| Payment reconciliation and compensation preserve one effect per operation key | R09, R10, R26, R33 |
| Committed events survive broker interruption, and replay converges with stored history | S01, S48, R14, R15, R32 |
| Orders waiting through an upgrade keep their recorded workflow path | R17 |
| Business commits continue during a workflow state-store outage, and workflow progress resumes after recovery | R07 |
| The reconciler detects and repairs faulted, missing, and mismatched workflows, with the expected alerts firing and clearing on a fresh stack | R30 focused rerun |

Functional acceptance ran on clean commit `cc02fe9`. The walkthrough
passed six of six checks. The full suite passed 78 of 80 steps in
95.2 minutes, including 270 unit tests in each build flavor and 218 SQL
Server integration tests. R20 and R30 then passed all five and ten
checks, respectively, on fresh drill replicas at the same commit.
The release preparation changes the version metadata, its identity
assertion, and the documentation. [Testing guide](../testing.md)

## Found on the Way

- **An approved order can finish before its signal retry.** In the full
  run, the workflow's timer read the stored decision and the order
  completed once. R20 read the signal before the next dispatch attempt.
  That attempt closed it as `Discarded/WorkflowTerminal`. On the fresh
  rerun, R20 observed `Forwarded` by the time it checked.
- **An earlier repair can keep a workflow-fault alert firing.** All three
  R30 repairs succeeded in the full run. An open case from R26 prevented
  the drill from attributing the alert to R30's own fault. The fresh
  run verified both alerts firing and clearing.

The original results and the causes are in the
[release-candidate journal entry](../journal.md#2026-11-24-release-candidate-verification).

## Known Limitations

- **Patched workflows can stall permanently after termination or purge**
  on the pinned Dapr 1.19.1 runtime. Use cancellation before dispatch and
  the order's review or intervention paths. A patched instance already
  stalled this way has no demonstrated automatic recovery.
  [Operations guide](../operations.md#never-terminate-a-patched-workflow)
- **A workflow-fault case can outlive a successful repair.** In R26 the
  reconciler opened a `WorkflowFaulted` case while a repair for another
  case was running. The repair voided the authorization, released the
  holds, and compensated the order. The fault case stayed open and kept
  the alert firing. [Journal](../journal.md#a-repaired-r26-order-kept-the-workflow-fault-alert-firing-in-r30)
- **Kubernetes evidence is limited to a smoke test.** Resilience under
  faults with mTLS and production high availability remain outside
  the demonstrated scope.
- **Operational and business bounds remain.** Workloads are synthetic,
  throughput figures are samples, and the timeline is a read model.
  A lost delivery-deadline timer still needs a separate wake-up. Payment
  and shipping exercise whole-order effects and simulated packing.
  [Guarantees and limits](../guarantees.md#partly-demonstrated-or-not-demonstrated)

## Deferred

- Kubernetes resilience drills with mTLS, including workflow-host loss
  and actor relocation.
- R28 variant B, which resets the Scheduler data volume during an outage.
  The Scheduler and Placement outage checks in variant A were exercised.

## Reproduce

The walkthrough and full suite recreate their local Compose test data.
Run them against a disposable evaluation stack.

```bash
bash scripts/init-env.sh
bash scripts/walkthrough.sh --fresh
bash scripts/verify.sh --suite full --keep-awake
# To repeat the two focused drills on a fresh drill stack:
bash scripts/verify.sh --suite full --only R20,R30 --keep-awake
```

Results under `artifacts/` include the Git commit, tree state, and observed
assertions. [Getting started](../getting-started.md)
